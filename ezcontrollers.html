Gaming should be EZ

EZ3 is a trackball gaming controller I created by combining a DualSense controller with a custom trackball rig, born out of my passion for gaming. It was originally designed to give players who prefer the comfort of a handheld controller a measurable advantage over traditional controllers by significantly increasing aiming accuracy, effectively narrowing the gap between controller users and keyboard-and-mouse players. I also discovered it benefits players who don’t have access to a mouse and keyboard. EZ3 was first unveiled at the 2024 Be More competition, hosted by NSHSS, where it won the top prize of $10,000 in funding. I continue to work on it whenever I can, and I have ambitious plans for its future.
